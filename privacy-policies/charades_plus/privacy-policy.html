Charades Plus – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how the Charades Plus mobile game ("the app") handles your information. The app is developed by Nikita Andreev ("we", "us").

In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Information stored on your device

To remember your preferences between games, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • player names you enter;
  • game settings (language, difficulty, turn length, number of rounds, sound and tilt options);
  • whether you have unlocked all themes.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. If you have turned on your device's own backup (for example, Android backup to your Google account), your operating system may include this data in that backup; this is controlled by your device settings, not by us.

Motion sensors

The app reads your device's motion sensor to detect when you tilt the phone to mark a word as guessed or skipped. Sensor readings are processed instantly on the device and are never recorded or transmitted.

In-app purchases

The app offers an optional one-time purchase that unlocks all themes. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. The store only tells the app whether the purchase was made. These stores handle your information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash reporting.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.
  • No sharing or selling of data to third parties.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Player names entered in the game stay on the device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
